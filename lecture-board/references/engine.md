# The engine: what the viewer gets

How a built page behaves. Read this before changing `assets/board.js` or `assets/board.css`, and when the user asks how something on the page works. Each point was agreed with the user; a change of behaviour is a change of this file too.

## Player
- No autoplay on opening: the first slide starts on Play or Space. Between slides the story flows: after a slide ends, a grey caption of its own names the next slide, offers a "Play next slide" button, and counts down (5 s by default, a setting), then starts the next slide.
- Autoplay is a switch in the control bar ("auto", key P). With autoplay the steps follow one another by themselves; without it the story stops after every step, its board drawn, Space plays the next step, and the next slide never starts by itself: the grey caption says "Space: play". Autoplay is on by default with voice and off without voice; each mode keeps the viewer's choice, and Reset restores the default.
- A slide whose first board action is `erase all` starts on a wiped board: the previous board and any figure controls fade out, and when playback starts from a finished slide a short pause (0.6 s) precedes the first caption; starting from a ready slide begins speaking at once. Until that erase runs, the board stays wiped, so the previous slide never shows through, also when the viewer moves with the arrows and has not pressed ▶.
- Jumping to a step rebuilds the board instantly from the latest `erase all` before it, not from the start of the talk; a figure taken off the board stops redrawing.
- Pause stops voice, animation and pauses together; an interrupted chunk is said again from its start. Opening a Why?, a Caution or an equation preview pauses the story until the card is closed; opening a definition does not.
- If the browser offers no voices, the voice button shows "!" and the story plays at reading pace. There is no watchdog for a stuck synthesizer: environments differ too much, and a watchdog made it worse; a browser restart is the remedy.
- While the story plays (and during the countdown), ← → never stop it: they play the previous or next step, across slide boundaries; in the countdown ← plays the slide's last step again and → starts the next slide. A → on a slide's last step stops on the grey caption, so fast stepping shows where a slide ends. While the story is stopped, ← → never start the voice: each step has two stops, first its caption over the board before it, then its board drawn with its animation (← goes back without animation); after a slide's last step comes the grey caption of the next slide. A → while a board is being drawn finishes the drawing instead of moving on. Space plays the step whose caption is shown if its board is not drawn yet, and the next step otherwise; R plays the current step again. Shift+← → (and the slide buttons) go to the start of the previous or next slide and keep the state.
- Show all (`⇒|`, key E; tooltip: Show full slide) silently finishes the current slide's board and shows its grey end caption without starting a countdown; repeating it stays on that slide. Next slide (`⏭`, Shift+→) is a separate action and goes to the beginning of the next slide. Speed 0.75×–2× in steps of 0.25 (`[` `]`).
- Keys: Space, ← → (or h l), E, Shift+← → (or k j), R, [ ], P, V, ? (open the Why?), ! (open the Caution), T (transcript), A (Read view), G (contents: ↑ ↓ choose a row, ← → previous / next part, Enter plays it, Esc closes), D (dark/light), F, S or ⌘, (settings), Esc. The settings drawer lists each key as "key — action". Letter keys are read by their physical position (`e.code`), so they work in any keyboard layout. A card page has no Read view, no Text toggle, and no `A`, `T`, `P`, `?` or `!` keys; its speed control (`Animation speed`) lives in Settings rather than in the control bar, while `⚙︎` stays in the control bar on the bottom right.
- The control bar is lean and stays on one row: previous slide, previous step ‹, Play, next step ›, Show all `⇒|`, next slide (thin chevrons for steps, so they never look like Play). With parts, the dots show only the slides of the current part; more than 10 dots become a bar of short segments.
- In a talk the heading above the board takes up to two rows: the optional section and part on the first row, the slide title and `Slide k/N · step i/m` on the second; missing hierarchy levels are omitted. The heading stays visible in full screen. A click on it, or G, opens the contents: the talk's title and subtitle at the top, then every section, part and slide, scrolled to the current one; a click plays that slide (or the part's first slide) from its start.
- Watch and Read sit with the settings button `⚙︎` in the top-right corner of a talk. A card page keeps its title and part tabs in the header and opens its subtitle on a click.
- The page remembers the place reached per script; a reload without `#n` returns to it, `#n` in the URL opens slide n.

## Two views of one text
- **Watch**: the board fits the window height (16:10, full screen on F). Under the drawing (a setting), on the board itself, a caption strip shows the current sentence (up to three lines); its glossary words open definitions without pausing, and a click elsewhere on it pauses and resumes. The transcript runs in a column on the right, as tall as the board, continuous across slides: past sentences muted, the current one bright and centred, the future hidden; a click on a sentence plays from it. A transcript under the board was tried and rejected as hard to follow.
- **Read** (A): the same text as an article, slide by slide, each with its finished board; Why? and Caution open inline after their sentence; a click on a sentence switches to Watch and plays from it.
- Side notes: in Watch the sign appears in the board's top right corner when its step is reached and stays to the end of the slide (Caution as a red triangle on the left, Why? as a yellow circle on the right); a one-line legend at the top of the transcript explains each sign the first time it appears; in the transcript the note's title is an indented line after its sentence.
- A card (definition, Why?, Caution, equation preview) opens next to the clicked word or at the top of the transcript column (a setting), and closes on Esc, on its ×, and on a click outside it.
- An equation reference (`[[eq:LABEL]]` or `\eqref{LABEL}`) opens the labelled board formula with its source slide title in a popup without leaving the current slide; multiline formulas with the same label on one slide appear together in script order.
- The shown text appears phrase by phrase with the voice; when the slide is done, all of it is visible.
- Later direction, not built: a Why? played as a detour with its own steps and board, and a conversation mode in which the viewer's questions become new branches.
- A figure slider is about 0.12em wide per value (6em to 16em), so a slow drag moves one value at a time; the wheel or a two-finger swipe over any slider, in a figure or in the settings, moves it by exactly one value. On figures (or individual controls) with `inspect: true`, moving a slider or clicking its `↻` replay button pauses Watch and previews that motion locally; pressing Play or changing steps restores the scripted state. Inspection controls stay hidden while a step's motion is armed or actively running.
- On a phone: figure sliders scale with the board; in landscape the controls and transcript sit beside a full-height board.

## Questions
- In a talk, a question (`Ask:`) uses the caption strip (or a temporary strip at the bottom of the board when captions are off) so the board and controls do not shift: prompt, answer input or click instruction, Check, Hint (when supplied), Show answer and Skip. A pending question holds ordinary forward playback (`Space`, `→`), while slide jumps and Show all remain available. After answering, revealing or skipping, Continue resumes playback, and Try again resets the question on that step.
- In Settings, **Questions** offers three modes: `ask`, `show the answers`, and `off`. Off omits question steps from navigation, speech, captions, transcript and Read while still running their board actions in order so subsequent steps have the board state they expect.

## Voice
- The default voice is the best one the browser offers for the script's language: names with "Premium", then "Enhanced", then "Natural", then the standard system voices; the viewer can pick another in the settings.
- Testing uses the Web Speech API (free, device-dependent). A recorded voice only for a final version.
- `voice: off` removes the voice, its button and its settings; each step then lasts its reading time (60 ms a letter, 90 ms a formula symbol, at least 1.8 s).

## Settings
- Each viewer tunes the page; the choice is kept in the browser (localStorage). The drawer opens on the right (S) in three groups (Display, Voice, Slides), then the keys, and Reset at the bottom.
- Page and board colours are two settings, each dark or light (D switches both). The board's colours are a separate set of tokens, not an inversion: on a light board the yellow becomes ochre and the blue and red darken.
- Text size (transcript and Read), board text size, caption size, board size; captions above the drawing, below it, or off; where cards open.
- In a talk, **Controls** offers **Auto-hide in full screen** (default: hides the control bar and top-right tools after 2.5 s of idle playback in full screen, waking on mouse, touch or key input), **Always show**, and **Hidden** (hides the control bar and `Watch / Read` switch so the board gets the space, while keeping `⚙︎` in the corner with quick buttons for Play/Pause, Contents, Read/Watch and Full screen).
- Defaults: page and board dark, transcript off, text 18 px, board text 100 %, captions 100 % below the drawing, controls auto-hide in full screen, questions ask, voice on, pauses 0.2 s for `|` and 0.4 s for `||`, pause between steps 0.3 s, next slide after 5 s.
- Everything fits on one screen without scrolling.

## Changing the engine
- A built page is self-contained (engine, KaTeX and figures inside), so a change of the engine never breaks a page already built; it can only break the next build of an old script.
- After an engine change, rebuild and run `scripts/lint.py` plus the browser regressions (`tests/player.py`, `tests/equations.py`) on the test fixtures in `tests/fixtures/`. When returning to an old talk, rebuild and lint it before editing it.
- Figure kinds live in each talk's `*.fig.js`. When a second talk needs a kind, copy it from the first and note it in `script-format.md`, so the library of kinds grows in one place.
